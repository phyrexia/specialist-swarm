# League of AI: brand kit

- `league-of-ai-share.jpg`: 1200x630 social card for Teams, Slack and link previews (cropped from the banner).
- `league-of-ai-square.jpg`: 1080x1080 avatar or post (the crest on a navy/teal glow background).
- `league-of-ai-crest.svg` / `.png`: hand-built vector crest and its 2048 px transparent render. Six emblems orbit a neural crystal: coordinator, explorer, flights, local insider, experiences, stays.
- `league-of-ai-banner.png`: 1536x1024 tournament key art. `league-of-ai-team-card.png`: 1024x1024 profile card. Both made with OpenAI `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst`; prompts are in `prompts.md`.

Palette: navy `#0B1D3A`, deep navy `#070F24`, teal `#14506A`, arcane blue `#39C8FF`, ice `#BFF3FF`, gold `#C89B3C`, bright gold `#F0D27A`, highlight `#FFF3C4`, bronze `#5A3D12`.

Font: Cinzel Bold (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts). In the SVG the letters are converted to paths, so it needs no installed font.

IP note: original artwork in a fantasy-esports style; not affiliated with Riot Games.
