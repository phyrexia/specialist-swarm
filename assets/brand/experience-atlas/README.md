# Experience Atlas: key visual kit ("Your AI travel companion", by League of AI)

- `experience-atlas-hero.png`: 1536x1024 key visual: golden-hour atlas, five travel moments, five agent orbs tracing the route; title and tagline rendered in the image.
- `experience-atlas-icon.png`: 1024x1024 app icon: compass rose plus globe with a five-node swarm route, gold-to-sunset on ocean blue.
- `experience-atlas-share.jpg`: 1200x630 social card (top crop of the hero, title intact). `experience-atlas-square.jpg`: 1080x1080 icon on a navy radial gradient with name, tagline and credit.
- The hero and the icon were made with OpenAI `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst`; prompts are in `prompts.md`. The JPGs were composed with Pillow.

Palette: deep navy `#060E20`, ocean blue `#1A3E68`, sunset amber `#D68C4C`, gold `#C89B50`, champagne `#F6E6C0`, mist `#C4D6E8`, slate `#8096B2`.

Font (square card): Didot Regular for the name, Avenir Next Regular/Medium for the tagline and credit (macOS system fonts). The hero title is lettering generated by the model.
